I’m Yichao.

My research focuses on systems and computer architecture for LLMs and other GenAI workloads, as well as high-performance data processing.

I’m a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Nishil Talati ↗. I also hold a B.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

About me

At UIUC, I work on systems and computer architecture for LLMs and other GenAI workloads.

My previous research focused on GPU-accelerated systems for large-scale data analytics and hardware–software co-design.

I began my Ph.D. at the University of Michigan and transferred to UIUC with my advisor, Prof. Nishil Talati ↗, in 2025. I also earned my M.S.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Michigan.
